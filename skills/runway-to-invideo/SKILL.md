# Runway clips to invideo

Read `/invideo` first.

1. Upload the clips. Look at each one.
2. Put them in order on a timeline. Trim each to its best part.
3. Add captions, music and voice (`/invideo-media`, `/invideo-generate`). Check the video, then export.
